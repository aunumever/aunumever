# Aunum ॐ

I build software for work driven by compassion. I've been doing it for over twenty years, starting with my own web studio in Ontario, Canada, then startups in Florida, including VP of UX, and CTO of an early conversational AI company in 2016. Vegan since 2011.

## Building now

**[Kindry](https://kindry.app)**
An operations platform for animal rescues and sanctuaries. It handles animal records, volunteers, donors, grants, and compliance, and it's free for every nonprofit rescue. Its AI layer, Hey Kindry, is a voice assistant built for the barn.

**Scuffle**
Software for adaptive sports leagues. It grew out of my years as a volunteer coach with a special needs hockey program.

Kindry and Scuffle are part of Humanologie Inc., built with React Native, Expo, Convex, and Cloudflare Workers.

**[Sustainable Support Team](https://sustainablesupport.org)**
I'm acting CTO for this Florida nonprofit, building its volunteer farm crew app. High school students run hydroponic towers at school, the harvest goes to a local food pantry every week, and each student leaves with an attested record of their service hours. It pilots in two schools in January 2027.

## Also

**[Sustainable Gardens](https://sustainablegardens.com)**
